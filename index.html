Active Chromewell Exhaust is a world class provider of exhaust and emissions solutions including thermal management technologies for off highway vehicles, equipment applications as well as for all gasoline, diesel and stationary engines.

Since 2013 this joint venture is a perfect combination of Active Exhaust Corporation's expertise in exhaust and thermal management system and Chromewell Engineering Limited having experise in broad metal fabrication capabilities and world class manufacturing practices.

Both in business for over 50 years and certified with ISO 9001 and TS16949

  2. - Established 1958
  3. - Design, test and manufacturing capabilities
  4. - Broad global OEM base
  5. - ISO-9001 certified
